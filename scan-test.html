Classifier accuracy check

Runs the real on-device classifier over Wikipedia photographs of 20 species that are in the roster, and reports what it said. This measures the model and the rollup, not the camera. It is an upper bound: these are curated photographs of the same kind the model was trained on — a phone pointed at a bee in long grass is harder. Needs a network connection for the photos. Everything prints to the log, so the whole result can be copied out of one page.
